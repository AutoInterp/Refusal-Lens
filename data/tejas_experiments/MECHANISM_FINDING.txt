TWO JAILBREAK MECHANISMS IDENTIFIED VIA CIRCUIT ATTRIBUTION

1. RP Jailbreaks (steerable): DAMPENING mechanism
   - Both pro-refusal and anti-refusal features decrease in magnitude
   - Circuit disengages from refusal computation
   - Steering can restore refusal because the circuit is quiet (easy to tip)

2. Fiction Jailbreaks (immune to steering): AMPLIFIED TUG-OF-WAR mechanism  
   - Both pro-refusal and anti-refusal features INCREASE massively
   - Circuit engages MORE with refusal, but anti-refusal scales to match
   - Steering fails because adding direction is noise vs existing 22+ vs 22+ battle
   - Fiction wrapping makes the model simultaneously detect harm AND generate content

Key evidence:
  Bare harmful:  pos~18.7  neg~-16.0  net~+2.7
  RP jailbreak:  pos~14.7  neg~-14.4  net~+0.4  (both decrease)
  Fiction:       pos~19.7  neg~-19.5  net~+0.2  (both INCREASE)

Same backbone features in all three: L8_p1_i498, L10_p1_i322, L9_p1_i291
The mechanism is magnitude, not different features.

Implication: Patching refusal via the refusal direction only works for dampening-type 
jailbreaks. Tug-of-war jailbreaks require a different intervention strategy.
